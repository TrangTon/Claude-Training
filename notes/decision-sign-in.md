## Question
Which sign-in should guests use to manage their booking?

## Options compared
| | Email + password | Google sign-in | Zalo login |
|---|---|---|---|
| Guest experience | Familiar, one more password | One click for most foreign guests | One click for most Vietnamese guests |
| Setup effort | Medium (reset flow, storing passwords) | Low to medium | Medium (Zalo developer account) |
| Ongoing cost | Low | Free | To check |
| Main risk | Storing passwords safely | Depends on Google | Less known to foreign guests |

## Recommendation
Google sign-in first, email + password as a fallback. Zalo in phase 2 if Vietnamese guests ask for it.

## Reasons against
- Two sign-in methods to support from day one.
- Some guests don't want to link a social account.
