# LS-108 Show the price before they book

GitHub: Issue #12

This ticket depends on LS-102 for the number of nights.

## User story
As a guest comparing rooms, I want to see the total price in VND as soon as I pick my dates and room type, so that I know what I will pay before I book.

## Rule
R4: total = nights × nightly rate. Standard is 600,000 ₫ a night and Deluxe is 900,000 ₫ a night.

## Acceptance criteria
| # | Given | When | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC1 | Standard, 10/11 → 11/11/2026 (1 night) | the dates and room type are valid | I see 1 night × 600,000 ₫ = 600,000 ₫ |
| AC2 | Standard, 10/11 → 13/11/2026 (3 nights) | the dates and room type are valid | I see 3 nights × 600,000 ₫ = 1,800,000 ₫ |
| AC3 | Deluxe, 10/11 → 12/11/2026 (2 nights) | the dates and room type are valid | I see 2 nights × 900,000 ₫ = 1,800,000 ₫ |
| AC4 | Deluxe, 10/11 → 17/11/2026 (7 nights) | the dates and room type are valid | I see 7 nights × 900,000 ₫ = 6,300,000 ₫ |
| AC5 | Deluxe, 30/01 → 02/02/2027 (3 nights, crossing a month) | the dates and room type are valid | I see 2,700,000 ₫ |
| AC6 | a price is showing for Standard, 3 nights | I switch to Deluxe | the price changes to 2,700,000 ₫ without reloading the page |
| AC7 | a price is showing | I change the guests from 2 to 4 | the price stays the same (guests do not affect the price) |
| AC8 | dates are missing or invalid (LS-102 errors), or no room type is chosen | I look at the price area | no number is shown, only "Choose dates and room type to see the price" |
| AC9 | someone changes the price in the browser to 1 ₫ before submitting | the server receives the booking | the server works out the price again from dates and room type, ignores the price it was sent, and saves the correct total |
| AC10 | any price is shown | I read it | it is a whole number in VND with thousands separators and the ₫ symbol, and no decimals |

## Scope
- Showing the price live on the booking form: nights, nightly rate and total.
- The nightly rates for Standard and Deluxe stored on the server in one place, not written into the page.
- The server working out the total again when the booking is submitted.

## Out of scope
- Taxes, service fees and VAT.
- Discounts, promo codes, and weekend, seasonal or holiday prices.
- Showing the price in other currencies (USD, KRW and so on) for foreign guests.
- Payment, deposits and cancellation fees.
- Checking whether the room is available.

## Open questions
- Do the rates include VAT? If not, should the form show VAT separately?
- Should the separator be a comma (1,800,000 ₫) or a dot as Vietnamese guests usually write it (1.800.000 ₫)? Should it change with the form language?
- Should foreign guests also see an approximate USD price? If so, where does the exchange rate come from?
- Is the rate per room per night, regardless of how many guests stay? (I assumed yes.)
- Who updates the rates, and how? Do we need an admin screen for that?
