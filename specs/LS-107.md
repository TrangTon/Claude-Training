# LS-107 Stop people booking past dates or a check-out before check-in

GitHub: Issue #11

## User story
As a guest planning a stay in Hoi An, I want the form to accept only valid check-in and check-out dates, so that I don't send a booking that the homestay will have to reject.

## Acceptance criteria
The examples use today = 02/11/2026, Vietnam time.

| # | Given | When | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC1 | today is 02/11/2026 | I choose check-in 02/11/2026 (today) | it is accepted (R2) |
| AC2 | today is 02/11/2026 | I choose check-in 01/11/2026 (yesterday) | I see "Check-in must be today or later", and past dates cannot be picked in the date picker |
| AC3 | check-in is 10/11/2026 | I choose check-out 11/11/2026 | it is accepted and nights = 1 (R3) |
| AC4 | check-in is 10/11/2026 | I choose check-out 10/11/2026 (same day) | I see "Check-out must be after check-in" and cannot submit |
| AC5 | check-in is 10/11/2026 | I choose check-out 08/11/2026 (earlier) | I see "Check-out must be after check-in" and cannot submit |
| AC6 | check-in 10/11 and check-out 12/11 are set | I change check-in to 15/11/2026 | check-out is cleared and I am asked to pick it again |
| AC7 | a guest in New York at 20:00 on 01/11, when it is already 08:00 on 02/11 in Vietnam | they choose check-in 01/11/2026 | it is rejected, because "today" is measured in Vietnam time |
| AC8 | I opened the form on 01/11 and left it open past midnight | I submit with check-in 01/11 | the server rejects it with "Check-in must be today or later" |
| AC9 | either date is empty | I press Submit | I see "Choose check-in and check-out dates" |
| AC10 | someone skips the browser checks | the server receives check-out ≤ check-in or a past check-in | it rejects the booking and saves nothing |
| AC11 | check-in is 30/01/2027 | I choose check-out 02/02/2027 | nights = 3 (month change handled correctly) |

## Scope
- Check-in and check-out date pickers, shown as DD/MM/YYYY.
- Rules R2 and R3, checked in the browser and on the server, with messages in English and Vietnamese.
- Working out the number of nights (check-out minus check-in, in calendar days) for LS-103 to use.

## Out of scope
- Room availability and stopping double bookings.
- A maximum length of stay, or a limit on how far ahead guests can book.
- Same-day booking cut-off times and check-in or check-out hours.
- Minimum stays on holidays or at Tết (Lunar New Year).

## Open questions
- Is there a latest time for same-day bookings, e.g. no check-in today after 22:00?
- What is the longest stay allowed, and how far ahead can guests book?
- Should foreign guests see the date as DD/MM/YYYY, or in the format their browser uses?
- Is it OK that the form uses Vietnam time even if the guest's own date is different?
