# LS-106 Guests must pick how many people are staying

GitHub: Issue #10

## User story
As a guest booking a room, I want to enter how many people are staying and see straight away if the number is not allowed, so that I only send a booking Lantern Stay can accept.

## Acceptance criteria
| # | Given | When | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC1 | the booking form is open | I enter 1 | the value is accepted and there is no error |
| AC2 | the booking form is open | I enter 6 | the value is accepted and there is no error |
| AC3 | the booking form is open | I enter 0 | I see "Enter 1 to 6 guests" and cannot submit |
| AC4 | the booking form is open | I enter 7 | I see "Enter 1 to 6 guests" and cannot submit |
| AC5 | the booking form is open | I enter 2.5 | I see "Number of guests must be a whole number" and cannot submit |
| AC6 | the booking form is open | I enter -1, abc or a space | I see "Enter 1 to 6 guests" and cannot submit |
| AC7 | the guests field is empty | I press Submit | I see "Number of guests is required" and the field is highlighted |
| AC8 | someone skips the browser checks and sends guests = 9 to the server | the server receives the booking | it rejects the booking with a validation error and saves nothing |
| AC9 | the form language is Vietnamese | any error above appears | the message shows in Vietnamese (e.g. "Vui lòng nhập từ 1 đến 6 khách") |
| AC10 | I showed an error | I change the value to a valid number | the error disappears without reloading the page |

## Scope
- The guests input on the booking form, using a number input or a 1–6 stepper.
- Checks in the browser and again on the server, with error messages in English and Vietnamese.
- Error text that screen readers can announce (linked to the field with aria-describedby).

## Out of scope
- Separate counts for adults and children, and age rules.
- Different guest limits for each room type.
- Extra-bed or extra-guest charges.
- Bookings for more than one room.

## Open questions
- Is 6 the limit per room, or per booking? Can a Standard room really hold 6 people?
- Do babies or children count toward the 6?
- Should the field start at 1, start at 2, or start empty?
- Should a value like "06" be accepted and treated as 6?
